title: Entries

An **entry** is the core unit of knowledge: Markdown content with a title, tags, and a source. Entries are searchable, linkable, and can own [attachments](/concepts/attachments).

## Lifecycle

An entry is either **active** or **archived** — that's the only lifecycle state. Archiving keeps an entry for the record without deleting it: archived entries are excluded from search and from the default entry listing, but stay readable by id and can be restored. Archive an entry with `POST /api/entries/{id}/archive`, restore it with `POST /api/entries/{id}/unarchive`. The `archived_at` field is a timestamp when archived, `null` otherwise.

## Scoping

Entries are scoped so a team — and multiple apps within it — can share one store without collisions:

- **Org** — your team/account boundary. Members share knowledge within the org. Roles: `owner` > `admin` > `editor` > `viewer`.
- **Workspace** — a broad app/product namespace, e.g. `support`, `engineering`, `general`.
- **Project** — a narrower work area within a workspace, e.g. a customer or product slug.

All of these are optional; a `NULL` field means "global". **Visibility:** you see your own entries, your org's entries, and global entries.

```json
{
  "title": "Refund policy",
  "content": "# Refunds\n\nFull refund within 30 days...",
  "tags": ["policy", "billing"],
  "workspace": "support",
  "project": "billing"
}
```

Writes require the `editor` role or above; reads respect org visibility. The same rules apply across the REST API, CLI, and MCP.
