An **entry** is the core unit of knowledge: Markdown content with a title, tags, a source, and a category. Entries are searchable, linkable, and can own [attachments](/concepts/attachments).

## Categories (PARA)

Every entry has a `category` from the [PARA](https://fortelabs.com/blog/para/) system:

| Category | For |
|----------|-----|
| `project` | Active, goal-bound work with an end state |
| `area` | Ongoing responsibilities with no end date |
| `resource` | Reference material and topics of interest (the default) |
| `archive` | Inactive items kept for the record |

## Scoping

Entries are scoped so a team — and multiple apps within it — can share one store without collisions:

- **Org** — your team/account boundary. Members share knowledge within the org. Roles: `owner` > `admin` > `editor` > `viewer`.
- **Workspace** — a broad app/product namespace, e.g. `reach`, `alertee`, `general`.
- **Project** — a narrower work area within a workspace, e.g. a customer or product slug.

All of these are optional; a `NULL` field means "global". **Visibility:** you see your own entries, your org's entries, and global entries.

```json
{
  "title": "Alertee positioning",
  "content": "# Wedge\n\nSimplicity is the wedge...",
  "category": "resource",
  "tags": ["reach", "positioning"],
  "workspace": "reach",
  "project": "alertee"
}
```

Writes require the `editor` role or above; reads respect org visibility. The same rules apply across the REST API, CLI, and MCP.
